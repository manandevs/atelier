Featured Models

Three Visions

Each model embodies a single aesthetic philosophy, showing the versatility and quiet innovation of contemporary couture.

01

Architectural Minimalism

Aria

Clean geometric lines meet sculptural silhouettes in this striking ensemble. The structured blazer features sharp angular cuts that create dramatic shadows, while the wide‑leg trousers flow with architectural precision. Monochromatic tones in dove grey and charcoal emphasise the garment's construction over ornamentation.

Silhouette
Oversized structured blazer with exaggerated shoulders, paired with high‑waisted wide‑leg trousers that create a commanding presence.
Fabrics
Premium wool gabardine with a subtle texture, reinforced with structured interfacing for architectural hold and movement.
Details
Invisible seaming, geometric pocket placement and minimalist hardware that disappears into the design's clean lines.
Styling
Sleek pointed‑toe boots, geometric jewellery and a structured handbag that echoes the architectural theme.
Enquire about Aria
02

Romantic Avant‑Garde

Luna

Ethereal layers and unexpected textures create a dreamlike vision of modern femininity. The asymmetrical dress features hand‑pleated silk organza that catches light like morning mist, while metallic threading adds subtle glamour. Soft blush and pearl tones evoke romance with a contemporary edge.

Silhouette
Asymmetrical midi dress with cascading layers, a fitted bodice and a flowing skirt that moves like liquid poetry with each step.
Fabrics
Silk organza, metallic tulle and hand‑embroidered mesh create depth and movement through varying levels of transparency.
Details
Hand‑sewn pearl beading, delicate feather trim and invisible boning that creates structure within the flowing design.
Styling
Strappy heeled sandals, delicate chain jewellery and a small beaded clutch that complements the dress's ethereal quality.
Enquire about Luna
03

Urban Futurism

Nova

Bold geometric patterns and innovative materials create a vision of tomorrow's fashion today. The statement jacket features laser‑cut panels and holographic accents, while the fitted trousers showcase technical fabrics that adapt to movement. Electric blues and metallic silvers reflect the energy of urban life.

Silhouette
Cropped bomber jacket with exaggerated proportions, paired with high‑waisted fitted trousers for a bold, futuristic profile.
Fabrics
Technical neoprene, holographic vinyl and moisture‑wicking performance fabrics that blend function with high fashion.
Details
Laser‑cut geometric patterns, LED‑integrated trim and magnetic closures that eliminate traditional hardware.
Styling
Platform trainers with metallic accents, geometric sunglasses and a structured backpack that doubles as a tech accessory.
Enquire about Nova
